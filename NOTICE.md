# Attribution

This site is a focused adaptation of the supplied AcademicPages archive
`academicpages.github.io-master.zip`, archive commit
`3d28cd27d0551b3d9dd8132f207538355fbbc7cc`.

AcademicPages descends from Michael Rose's Minimal Mistakes theme. The original
MIT license and its copyright notice are retained in `LICENSE`.
The light theme palette, system-font family, masthead, author profile, archive
presentation, caption treatment, and muted footer informed this adaptation.
The original names `.masthead`, `.masthead__inner-wrap`, `.sidebar`,
`.author__avatar`, `.page__content`, and `.page__footer` are retained for continuity.

Layout and CSS were simplified to remove Sass vendor grids and the JavaScript
build chain, not to introduce a different design system. Placeholder SVGs and
thesis/paper components are specific to this adaptation. No original font files,
photos, demo publications, or scientific content are redistributed.

MathJax is referenced from a version-pinned external CDN and is not vendored.
Scientific content and media inserted later may have their own licensing;
review their distribution rights separately from the template code.
